Privacy policy

Last updated: Nov 23, 2026.

In short

You do not need an account to browse. Logging in with Discord is optional. The site sets a cookie only if you log in, does not use advertising or tracking scripts and does not ask for more personal data than described below.

Logging in with Discord (optional)

  • What we receive. When you log in, Discord gives us your Discord ID, username, display name and avatar (the "identify" permission). We do not receive your e-mail, your servers or your messages, and we cannot act on your Discord account. We do not keep the access token Discord issues.
  • What we store. Those four items, the date of your first and latest login, and, in the future, your likes and the skins you upload. We use them to show who you are, to link skins to their author and to enforce usage limits.
  • Cookies. Only strictly necessary ones, set only when you log in: a session cookie (sd_session, up to 30 days, HttpOnly) and a short-lived login check cookie (sd_state, 10 minutes). They are not used for advertising or tracking. Logging out removes the session cookie.
  • Legal basis. Your request to log in, to provide the account features (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7, V), and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.
  • Deleting your account. Write to our contact address and we will delete your account data and, if you ask, your skins.

What is processed when you visit

  • Hosting and security. The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and requested pages to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Clipboard. The "Copy code" button writes the skin code to your clipboard only when you click it.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from third parties.

Information about skin authors

Each skin page shows the skin's appearance code, a preview image and the author's public Discord username, taken from posts the author made in the game's public community channel. We do not collect any other information about authors. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing public community creations with credit (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX), balanced against your right to object.

Your rights

You can ask us to remove a skin, or your username, to correct it or to tell you what we hold about you, by writing to our contact address or using the removal request page. We act on removal requests quickly (see that page).

Third-party links

Links to Steam, Discord and other sites are governed by their own policies.

Changes

If the site adds advertising or analytics, or starts to use your data in a new way (such as uploads and likes), this policy will be updated before the feature goes live and, where required, consent will be requested.
